Portfolio briefing for Indian equities

Read what companies file. Check what they said.

FINN turns exchange filings into a short daily briefing, and answers research questions only from a company's annual report and earnings call, with the quote, page and original document behind every claim.

Try the demo

  • Demo sign-in only. There is no password and no account. It is not a secure login.
  • Your name is saved only in this browser, on this device. It is never sent to FINN's server.
  • Anyone using this browser can see it. Sign out or change it any time in Profile.

Today

Prioritised exchange filings for your own portfolio (it starts with 16 demo companies you can change), with compact volume and price signals. Each source is marked live or saved, with its time.

Research

Ask about any NSE company. Answers come only from its latest annual report and earnings-call transcript, and each claim opens its supporting quote.

Honest about limits

End-of-day prices are labelled as such. Guidance is only compared with results when metric, period and scope match.
